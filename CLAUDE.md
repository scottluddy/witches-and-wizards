# Witches and Wizards: wizard school RPG

A family project: Scott drives, his two 9-year-old daughters make the art and design the content. Keep everything simple and readable, so a parent can follow the code and the kids can edit content.

## Rules

- Plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no libraries, no build step.
- Must run by double-clicking `index.html` (file://) and deploy to Netlify as static files.
- All game content (characters, locations, paths, items, levels) lives in `/data`. Never hard-code content in JS.
- Data files are `.js` files holding JSON-style content with one wrapper line, e.g. `GAME_DATA.characters = [ ... ];`, loaded by `<script>` tags. This is so double-clicking index.html works (browsers block reading `.json` from file://). Scott chose this on 2026-10-07.
- Progress is saved in `localStorage` as a list of up to 10 saved games (newest first, `js/save.js`); the title screen has a dropdown to continue one, and the 💾 Switch Game button in the top bar saves and goes back to the title screen. "New Game" adds a new game and, when the list is full, removes the oldest after asking (Scott, 2026-10-10). Saves belong to one browser and one web address.
- The player chooses Witch or Wizard (`player.kind`) at the start; progress is Levels starting at 1, shown as "Witch Level 1" (Scott, 2026-10-10; was Grades).
- Going up a Level (Scott, 2026-10-11, a sample plan): `data/levels.js`, `js/levels.js`. Each new Level needs 3 more each of Adventure Points, Chore Points and Bonding Points, and 3 more Skills (skill Levels plus spells, `player.skills` + `player.spells`; 3 Levels of one skill or 1 each of three, Scott 2026-10-13) since the last Level (Scott, 2026-10-11: fish and dances don't count; fish are for food and spells, dancing is just for fun) (`player.levelStart` keeps the counters from then), and costs 10 gems × your Level. Clicking the Level in the top bar opens a pop-up with progress bars and a Level Up button. Powers: each Level above 1 adds 5% luck (Claude's sample; Scott wants Levels to make you stronger, not decided how). A `needs` id can name a number or a list (a list counts its length). The Adventure stars in the top bar count toward the next Level.
- Luck (Scott, 2026-10-11): `data/luck.js`, `js/luck.js`. The Luck Potion (`potion_luck`, Trading Post) has a 🍀 Drink button in the backpack: +25% luck for 5 minutes of play (`player.luckSeconds`, 🍀 4:59 in the top bar). Luck makes chances of good things bigger (a 40% chance becomes 50%): a fish bites more, the Crystal Dig Spot finds a Crystal more, a woken troll may stay asleep. Use `luckyChance`/`secondChance` for any new chance of success.
- Wands and timed potions (Scott, 2026-10-18, the girls' drawings with gem prices; photos art/raw/sheets/*_wand.jpeg, invisibility_potion.jpeg, wisdom_potion.jpeg): `data/wands.js`, `js/wands.js`, `js/potions.js`. On the Campus Store's Wand shelf: Healing Wand 13, Lightning Wand 15, Music Wand 8 gems. Each has a little picture (`wand_*_magic`) that flies out of its tip when used (`waveWand`); a wand works from the backpack and then rests `restMinutes` (`player.wandRest`, Claude's guesses: 30, 10, 5) instead of running out. Healing: at the Crafting Table it fills in for up to 3 missing materials (`materialsShort`; never the Crystal or the potion; a 💖 on the toss button). Lightning: when a troll steals Crystals, a ⚡ Zap button in the troll pop-up; it drops them (they come back) and runs off (`trollDropsIt`). Music: in a Piano test, a button to use it, then it fixes up to 3 wrong notes (`musicWandHelps`). On the Potion shelf: Invisibility Potion 5 (`potion_invisible`; the Trading Post's `potion_invisibility` works the same), invisible for 1 minute: you shimmer, a woken troll can't see you to steal, bees can't sting you. Wisdom Potion 6 (`potion_wisdom`), for 5 minutes a passed Wizard School test needs half the rest before the next (Claude's choice of "smarter in school"). Both 🧪 Drink in the backpack and count down in the top bar (`player.potionSeconds`, only while playing).
- Show the player's own character on screen wherever possible (Scott, 2026-10-17): walking into a room, sitting at a table or on a couch, meeting other characters. Skip it only where it would get in the way of playing. Maps already show it. A spot with `sitAt` (x, y of your feet, `size` = height in percent) sits you down there when clicked, until you leave the place (`sitYouAt` in js/world.js): the Cozy Lounge sofa, the Cafe armchair (on its menu spot), the Museum Snack Shop chair and the Pond House couch. You also stand in every place you walk into (`standYouIn` in js/world.js, `data/standing.js`: the bottom-left corner unless `places` gives another spot; `skip` lists places where you'd be in the way, plus all shelves). The Flea Market does too (you walk to the booth and sit at your table, `walkYou` in js/market.js) and the roller coaster car carries it. Dragging and couches (Scott, 2026-10-18): `js/you.js`. Drag your character anywhere in a place's picture; once dragged or seated you're half the size you walk in at (`movedSize` in data/standing.js, Scott 2026-10-18: so you don't cover the drawings), and you hang from the mouse by your middle; higher up you get smaller (`smallest` in data/standing.js) so you look further back. Click a couch or chair, or drop yourself on it, to sit there sized to fit: furniture pieces with `seat` in data/furniture.js (x, y across the piece, `size` your height; you move with the piece if it's dragged) and spots with `sitAt` (dropping counts as clicking). Dragging off a seat stands you up. Sharing seats (Scott, 2026-10-18): `sitters` on a seat (2 for couches, else 1); one sitter sits in the middle, two sit `shareShift` either side (you on the left, the little WIT on the right, `seatPlace` in js/you.js, `othersSittingOn` in js/windy.js); a full seat says so. Little WITs are always half your size (standing: half your moved size at that spot; sitting: half your size on that seat). In the Snowy Hut, when no little WIT is being rescued, sit down and drag the blanket onto you (`blanketForYou` in js/windy.js). Clicking yourself does nothing (so it never digs the cave wall or searches for pearls underneath).
- Zooming (Scott, 2026-10-17, "feel like you're inside it"): going into a place, from a map or a bigger place (store → shelf), zooms the picture in on its spot until it fills the box, then the new drawing fades in; the 🔍 Zoom out to ... button (was Back) starts zoomed in on the spot you left and zooms out (`js/zoom.js`; everything on a picture sits in `#map-zoom` / `#place-zoom`). The title shows as a caption on arrival, then fades (`flashTitle`). Skipped when the computer asks for less motion. Scott's idea of clicking anywhere to zoom into a quarter was left out (Claude: it fights the click-anywhere activities like mining, pearls and the acorn search, blurs the photos and doubles the clicks); ask him before adding it.
- Map positions are percentages (x, y) of the map image, so they survive resizing.
- Desktop game for now (Scott, 2026-10-10: no need to design for phones). Map and place pictures grow to fill the window's width or height, whichever runs out first (`.picture` in css/style.css, `--shape` set in js/world.js).
- Style: whimsical and magical, parchment backgrounds, friendly storybook font, big kid-friendly buttons.
- Short functions, plain names, a comment wherever a 9-year-old's parent might wonder "why".
- Build in stages and stop after each one so the family can test. See `PLAN.md`.

## Game balance (Scott, 2026-10-11)

- Crystals are rare: any easy way to get them has a low but positive expected return (more found than the troll steals, but not by much).
- Fishing is the main way to earn Gems; other ways (digging, dancing) should earn less per minute. Check the catalog's Game Play Logic earning table after changing numbers.
- Nothing pays twice: catches sell for half, Scholarship items can't be sold.
