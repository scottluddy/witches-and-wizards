          Ideas

          Suggestions only. None of these are in the game. Draw one, give it a price and move it from data/ideas.js to data/items.js to put it in the store.

          Fish

          Caught with the Lucky Rods at the Dock after a wait of 5 to 25 seconds (a wait past 20 seconds catches nothing). Each one goes in the cooler you carry (empty it into the cooler at Home) and earns gems.

          Treasures

          Other things the Lucky Rods pull up. They earn gems too, but they don't count as fish (listed in data/fish.js under treasures).

          Beach finds

          Dug up at the Dig Spot on Pebble Beach (listed in data/digging.js). They go in your bucket, which you can empty into the treasure chest at Home. The Crystal Dig Spot finds Crystals instead.

          Crystal Cave finds

          Mined in the Crystal Cave with a pickaxe from the rack (listed in data/mining.js). Crystals add to your Crystals; bones go in your satchel, which you can empty at the Spell Book Table at Home.

          Pearl Cave finds

          Found in the water of the Pearl Cave, behind the Pearl Waterfall (listed in data/pearls.js). Click the water to search. They go in your bucket (empty it into the treasure chest at Home) and are spell ingredients.

          ⚙️ Game Play

          Good things to do

          Activities that reward you for good things: Bonding (caring for your pet), Chores (tidying up), Mining (gathering things) and Learning (like the maths puzzle at the Mushroom Ring). Ideas go in data/gameplay.js first, then get built into the right places in the game.

          Metrics

          The counters in the bar at the top of the game: how you earn each one and what it's for (also in data/gameplay.js).

          ⚙️ Game Play Logic

          How the game fits together, to help spot things that don't make sense, like one place earning gems much faster than another. Most of this is worked out from the game's own data, so it stays up to date; the rules and bags are in data/logic.js.

            Adding something new

            1. Places live in data/places.js, characters in data/characters.js, items in data/items.js.
            2. Photograph a drawing and save it in art/raw with the file name shown here.
            3. Run python3 tools/process_art.py. The drawing replaces the placeholder and its tag turns green.
